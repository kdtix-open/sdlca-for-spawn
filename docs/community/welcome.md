# Welcome

This page is for anyone thinking about joining the SDLCA agent kit community, whether you build games on Spawn, write code, are new to open source, or are simply curious. It shows who is welcome, how you can help without writing code, what a first week can look like, and how we treat each other.

## Hello

The kit is a set of small tools that let you direct AI coding agents on Spawn from chat. You run it on your own computer, and it works for you. It is an unofficial community project, maintained by KDTIX Open. It is not made or supported by Spawn. It is also new and small, so there is plenty of room for you to shape it.

You do not need anyone's permission to take part. You do not need to be a programmer. You do not need to have used git: [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#your-first-contribution-in-30-minutes) starts with a way to propose a change from your web browser. You do not even need to run the kit yourself. If you have an opinion about what is confusing, missing or wrong, we want to hear it.

## Who is welcome

- **Spawn creators who do not write code.** You know what it is like to make a game by talking to Savi. You can tell us where our words lose you, and that is some of the most useful feedback we can get.
- **Developers and people who run AI agents.** Bring your questions, your bug reports and your patches.
- **Players and playtesters.** If you have tried a world that an agent helped build, tell us how it went.
- **People new to open source.** This is a good place for a first contribution. We aim to keep a few small, well-described starter issues for you, and we explain our reasons.
- **People from other communities.** If you come from an agent tool, a chat platform or another project, you are welcome too.
- **People who write in other languages, and people who need the project to be more accessible.** Tell us what gets in your way. See [communication](communication.md).

We assume nothing about your experience, and nobody here will make you feel small for asking.

## Ways to help that are not code

Every one of these matters, and we say thank you for every one:

- **Try it and tell us what happened.** Follow [getting started](../getting-started.md), then tell us where you got stuck.
- **Read the docs as a newcomer.** Mark every sentence you had to read twice. Open a "Documentation improvement" issue, or fix it yourself.
- **Answer a question** in Discussions. If you just learned something, you are the best person to explain it.
- **Report a bug** with the smallest steps that show it. That is a gift to the next person.
- **Check our claims about Spawn** against Spawn's own pages, because Spawn changes its rules often. Our [monthly check](../contributing/releasing.md#the-monthly-check-of-spawns-terms) welcomes volunteers.
- **Test on a system we have not tried.** We have tested only on Ubuntu under WSL2. The CI job is set up to run the same checks on GitHub's Ubuntu runner.
- **Help with triage.** Reproduce a bug, suggest a label, or point out a duplicate. [Triage and labels](triage-and-labels.md) explains how we sort things.
- **Review for accessibility and plain language.** Short sentences, good descriptions of images, and links that say where they go.
- **Translate.** We have no translation workflow yet. Open an idea in Discussions first, so we can agree how to organize it. See the [roadmap](roadmap.md).
- **Show what you built** in the Show and tell category.

## What a first week can look like

This is only a suggestion. Take as long as you like, and skip anything you do not want.

| Day | You could... |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Read this page and [getting started](../getting-started.md). Say hello in Discussions if you like. |
| 2 to 3 | Try the kit, or read the docs without running anything. Write down what confused you. |
| 4 | Tell us. Post the confusing part in Q&A, or open a "Documentation improvement" issue. |
| 5 to 6 | If you want to go further, pick something small, such as a typo or a starter issue labeled `good first issue`. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) has a path that takes about 30 minutes. |
| 7 | We aim to reply to anything you opened within about 7 days. If you hear nothing after 14 days, a polite nudge is welcome. |

If you get stuck on any step, ask. Nobody minds, and your question helps the next person.

## How we treat each other

The [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies to everyone, maintainers included. In plain words:

- Be kind. Assume good faith, and ask before you assume the worst.
- Talk about the work and the ideas, never about the person.
- Give credit, and say thank you.
- Say "I do not know" and "I got that wrong". Both are welcome here.
- Be honest about AI. We build with AI agents and welcome AI-assisted work. We only ask you to say when it helped. See [AI-assisted contributions](../contributing/ai-assisted-contributions.md).
- Keep private things private. Never post tokens, invite codes, other people's messages, or any other session, join or login credential, including values printed by `spawn client join`.
- When we say no, we say why. A "no" or "not yet" is about the change, never about you.

If something goes wrong, write to Open.Source@kdtix.com. The reporter's privacy is protected.

## How recognition works

We keep this simple and honest.

- **Release notes.** GitHub generates our release notes from merged pull requests, and each entry names the person who opened it. We read the draft before we publish it, so tell us if you would rather not be named, or want to be named differently.
- **The history.** Your commits stay in the project's history under your name.
- **Security reporters** are credited in the advisory if they want to be. See [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
- **Roles.** If you keep helping, you can become a reviewer or a maintainer. [GOVERNANCE.md](../../GOVERNANCE.md) explains how.

That is all we promise. We have no badges, no contributor wall, no swag and no paid roles. Work that does not go through a pull request, such as answering questions, testing and reporting, does not appear in the notes automatically. We thank you in the thread instead, and we mean it.

## A note for the Spawn community

This project is independent. It uses the doors that Spawn publishes for agents and for the people who run them, and it follows Spawn's published rules, which you can read in [the rules we follow](../compliance/spawn-rules.md). We do not speak for Spawn, and nobody at Spawn has reviewed or endorsed the kit.

- **Feedback about Spawn belongs with Spawn.** Bugs in a world, Savi or the app go to Spawn's own channels, and anything you send Spawn may become Spawn's. [Feedback and ideas](feedback-and-ideas.md) says where to send it and what to know first.
- **Spawn's rules come first.** If you mention the kit in a Spawn space, read that community's rules, say plainly that the kit is unofficial, and never suggest Spawn approves it. We do not advertise in Spawn's spaces, and we ask you not to do it for us.
- **Your worlds stay yours.** Please never paste another creator's world code or assets, your tokens, or any other session, join or login credential, including values printed by `spawn client join`, into anything you post here.
- **Read Spawn's Terms yourself.** Spawn's Terms set a minimum age and parental-consent rules and contain a sanctions clause (read 2026-11-17). The person who runs the kit must meet them. We do not interpret them. Spawn rooms can include young people, and your agent forwards what it reads there to your model provider.
- **Spawn changes fast,** and so does this kit. If something stops matching, tell us with the "Spawn changed and the kit broke" form.
